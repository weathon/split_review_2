## Human Reviewer 3

### Questions
On page 6, the authors state that *"ICLR demonstrates a broader distribution of replies than NeurIPS"* and argue that this *"reflects a more dynamic, iterative review environment."* The question here is: why is *a broader distribution of replies* considered inherently beneficial? Would a higher number of replies generally be regarded as a positive factor? The logical connection in this argument could be strengthened. More broadly, while the paper's main claim—that the peer review process should be more open and transparent—is reasonable and aligns with ongoing efforts to improve research integrity and reviewer-author communication, the reasoning could be more rigorously substantiated. The reviewer agrees that openness can enhance transparency and help reviewers better understand authors' intentions, which is beneficial for the research community. However, the paper would be more solid if it provided more concrete evidence or a more complete logical chain to substantiate the claim.
