## Summary
This paper introduces PiCO, a novel unsupervised evaluation method for large language models (LLMs) that leverages a peer-review mechanism. The core idea is to have LLMs evaluate each other's responses to unlabeled questions, with each model assigned a learnable capability parameter that influences the weight of its evaluation. The goal is to optimize the consistency between a model's capability score and the scores it receives from peer reviews, thereby ranking models in a way that aligns with human preferences. The authors validate PiCO on three datasets: Chatbot Arena, MT-Bench, and AlpacaEval, using three newly proposed metrics: PEN, CIN, and LIS, which measure the alignment between model rankings and human preferences. The experimental results demonstrate that PiCO outperforms several baseline methods, suggesting its effectiveness as an unsupervised evaluation approach. The paper's main contribution lies in its innovative use of peer review among LLMs to achieve unsupervised evaluation, reducing the reliance on human annotations. The authors propose a constrained optimization framework based on the consistency assumption, which posits that high-capability LLMs can provide more accurate evaluations and also achieve higher answer-ranking scores. The method involves a two-stage process: first, LLMs evaluate each other's responses, and second, the model capabilities are optimized to align with the evaluation scores. The paper also introduces three new metrics, PEN, CIN, and LIS, to quantify the alignment between model rankings and human preferences. The empirical findings show that PiCO achieves better alignment with human rankings compared to baseline methods across the three datasets. The authors also conduct ablation studies to validate the consistency assumption and the effectiveness of the proposed optimization framework. Overall, the paper presents a significant step towards unsupervised LLM evaluation, offering a promising alternative to human-based evaluation methods. However, as I will detail in the following sections, there are several limitations that need to be addressed to fully realize the potential of this approach.

## Strengths
The primary strength of this paper lies in its innovative approach to unsupervised LLM evaluation. The idea of using LLMs to evaluate each other's responses, without relying on human annotations, is a significant contribution to the field. This approach has the potential to reduce the cost and time associated with human-in-the-loop evaluation, making it a more scalable and practical solution. The introduction of a learnable capability parameter for each LLM is also a novel aspect of the method. This parameter allows the system to dynamically adjust the weight of each model's evaluation based on its perceived capability, which is a clever way to address the varying quality of LLMs. The constrained optimization framework, based on the consistency assumption, is another notable technical innovation. The authors propose that high-capability LLMs can provide more accurate evaluations and also achieve higher answer-ranking scores, and they use this assumption to guide the optimization process. The experimental results, which demonstrate that PiCO outperforms several baseline methods across three datasets, provide strong empirical support for the effectiveness of the proposed approach. The use of three newly proposed metrics, PEN, CIN, and LIS, to quantify the alignment between model rankings and human preferences, is also a valuable contribution. These metrics provide a way to objectively measure the performance of the evaluation method. Furthermore, the ablation studies, which validate the consistency assumption and the effectiveness of the proposed optimization framework, add to the robustness of the paper. The paper's clear and well-structured presentation also contributes to its overall strength. The authors provide a detailed description of the method, the experimental setup, and the results, making it easy for the reader to understand the core concepts and findings. The inclusion of an appendix with additional details further enhances the paper's clarity and completeness. In summary, the paper's strengths lie in its innovative approach, technical advancements, strong empirical results, and clear presentation, making it a valuable contribution to the field of LLM evaluation.
