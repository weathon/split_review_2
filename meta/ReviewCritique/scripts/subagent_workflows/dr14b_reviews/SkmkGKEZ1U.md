### Summary

The paper introduces O-Forge, a tool designed to assist in proving asymptotic inequalities by combining large language models (LLMs) with a computer algebra system (CAS). The tool aims to address the challenge of verifying complex mathematical proofs, particularly in research-level mathematics. The authors demonstrate the tool's effectiveness through case studies, including problems posed by mathematician Terence Tao.
