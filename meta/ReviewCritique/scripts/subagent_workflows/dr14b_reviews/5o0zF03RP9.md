### Summary

The paper proposes a novel framework called IncentRL, which integrates external rewards with internal incentives through KL-based preference prediction alignment. The key innovation is treating the incentive weight as a Bayesian random variable, updated online to balance exploration and exploitation without manual tuning. The framework augments task rewards with a KL-based penalty that aligns predicted outcome distributions with preferred outcomes. Theoretically, this connects to dopamine-based reward prediction error and the Free Energy Principle. Empirically, on MiniGrid and MountainCar, IncentRL improves sample efficiency and final performance over standard RL and fixed-regularization baselines.
