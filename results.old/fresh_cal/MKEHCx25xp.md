## Summary

WildBench introduces a benchmark of 1,024 challenging real-user tasks from the WildChat dataset, with careful curation ensuring balanced task distribution across categories. It provides two automated evaluation metrics — WB-Reward (pairwise, using three baselines) and WB-Score (individual scoring) — both employing instance-specific checklists and a length penalty to improve LLM-as-a-judge reliability. The metrics demonstrate strong correlation with Chatbot Arena human Elo ratings, surpassing ArenaHard and AlpacaEval2.0 on hard tasks.

## Strengths

- **Carefully curated real-world data with balanced task distribution.** The 1,024 tasks are sourced from over one million WildChat conversations through multi-stage filtering (length, diversity, difficulty annotation by three LLMs, human review). The resulting distribution (Figure 2, Table 1) is substantially more balanced across categories than AlpacaEval (over 50% information-seeking) or ArenaHard (over 57% coding/debugging), directly supporting the goal of mirroring real-world usage.

- **Instance-specific checklists improve evaluation interpretability and reliability.** Each task has 5–10 manually reviewed checklist questions generated by GPT-4-Turbo and Claude-3-Opus. The ablation (Section 4.3) demonstrates that checklists improve Pearson correlation from 0.905 to 0.925 with human preferences. This is a concrete, measurable contribution over plain LLM-as-a-judge.

- **Length-bias mitigation with a simple, customizable margin.** Converting "slightly better/worse" to "tie" when the winner's response exceeds the loser's by K characters is a clean and intuitive approach. The ablation confirms K=500 as optimal, and the paper provides evidence that longer outputs do not automatically rank higher (e.g., Llama-2-70B-chat and Yi-1.5-6B have long outputs but low ranks).

- **Two complementary metrics with different cost-quality trade-offs.** WB-Reward (pairwise, three baselines, more expensive) and WB-Score (individual scoring, ~$5 for 10 minutes) are both validated and made available, allowing users to choose based on their needs. The leaderboard provides both, which is practical.

- **Dynamic updates with data leakage prevention.** Coordinating with the WildChat team to ensure sampled tasks are not publicly released addresses contamination risks that affect static benchmarks. Two versions (V1 March 2024, V2 May 2024) have already been released.

- **Strong overall correlation with human judgment.** Both WB-Reward and WB-Score achieve high Pearson correlations with Chatbot Arena Elo (0.98 and 0.95 respectively on top-ranking models), outperforming ArenaHard (0.91) and AlpacaEval2.0 (0.87/0.89).
