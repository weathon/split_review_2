## Novel Insights

The paper's most practical finding — that graph-based RAG (LightRAG) and web-search RAG (CRAG) are unsuitable for role-playing due to entity ambiguity and persona-polluting web results respectively — is genuinely informative for the RAG-RPA research community, even though it is presented as an empirical result rather than a central contribution. The CharacterRAG dataset (character personas reconstructed in first-person, with out-of-context editorial content manually removed) represents a methodologically thoughtful contribution to the benchmark landscape. However, the paper's main technical insight — that inferential chunk selection and attribute extraction improve out-of-knowledge persona consistency — remains incompletely validated due to the model-asymmetry problem and absent ablation.
