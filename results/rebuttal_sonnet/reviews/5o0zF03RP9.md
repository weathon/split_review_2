## Summary

IncentRL augments RL rewards with a KL-divergence penalty between a predicted outcome distribution p(o|s,a) and a preferred outcome distribution q(o|s), weighted by β. The paper's primary claim is that β is treated as a Bayesian random variable updated online, eliminating manual tuning. Experiments cover a two-state toy MDP (7 seeds), MountainCar-v0 (3 seeds), and MiniGrid DoorKey 8×8 (3 seeds). The theoretical analysis consists of two propositions characterizing β's boundary behavior.

## Novel Insights

The idea of framing the intrinsic-extrinsic trade-off parameter as a Bayesian random variable adapted online is a genuinely interesting conceptual contribution that bridges reward shaping with Bayesian inference. If properly specified and evaluated, this could provide a principled alternative to grid-search or meta-gradient β tuning. However, in the current paper this idea is described but never operationalized, making it impossible to assess whether it is novel, correct, or practically useful. The fixed-β KL shaping mechanism is straightforward and closely related to existing KL-regularized RL (TRPO, SAC), differentiated primarily by acting on outcome distributions rather than policies — a distinction the paper draws but does not experimentally isolate.
