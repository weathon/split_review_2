## Summary

## Novel Insights

The most genuine contribution is the observation that SAM's decoder output tokens — normally used only as an intermediate representation for mask prediction — are semantically richer and more instance-discriminative than the image encoder's embeddings for the purpose of counting-by-matching. The mechanism is sound: output tokens accumulate both semantic content and prompt-conditioned positional context through bidirectional attention, producing representations that separate target instances from background more cleanly than cosine similarity over raw image features. This insight is substantiated by the ablation in Table 5 and qualitatively by Figure 5, and is portable to other SAM-based zero-shot tasks. The probabilistic prompt generation and TS-SS metric are supportive engineering choices rather than primary innovations.
