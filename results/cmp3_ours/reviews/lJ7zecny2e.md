## Summary

This paper introduces RSThinker, a vision-language model for remote sensing that produces perceptually-grounded chain-of-thought (Geo-CoT) reasoning traces. The framework has three components: (1) Geo-CoT380k, a 384k-sample dataset of structured rationales generated by GPT-4V conditioned on ground-truth annotations; (2) a two-stage training pipeline (SFT to instill the reasoning structure, then GRPO to refine it); and (3) the resulting RSThinker model. Empirical results show large and consistent improvements across visual grounding, object counting, detection, classification, and VQA benchmarks, with compelling zero-shot generalization.

## Strengths

1. **Problem framing is precise and well-grounded.** The paper articulates a genuine gap—VLMs produce unverifiable outputs—and makes a specific, compelling case for spatially-anchored reasoning in high-stakes remote sensing (disaster response, environmental monitoring). The distinction between being *correct* and being *verifiably* correct is well-articulated in Section 1.

2. **Empirical results are unusually strong and consistent across a broad suite.** RSThinker achieves 90.4% @0.5 on VRSBench-VG (vs. 63.8% for the best baseline, Table 4), 85.26% Acc on HRRSD object counting (vs. 61.48%, Table 5), and strong zero-shot generalization on RRSIS-D, RSOD, and NWPU-VHR where no training data was used. The improvement is systematic across tasks—not a single outlier—which strengthens the case that the framework is doing something real.

3. **The ablation study (Table 8) is properly structured.** It cleanly separates SFT without CoT, SFT with CoT, and GRPO on top of each, making the causal role of structured rationales visible. The design allows readers to see which component does what.

4. **The failure analysis (Figure 7) is intellectually honest.** Showing a structurally sound but factually wrong reasoning trace, and arguing why explicit grounding still makes this preferable to opaque errors, demonstrates genuine engagement with the framework's limitations.

5. **Geo-CoT380k is a concrete, reusable contribution.** At ~384k samples across VQA, grounding, detection, counting, classification, and captioning, it is a substantial resource that will likely benefit the community independently of the model.

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None.

### Major

1. **The SFT data generation pipeline conditions on ground-truth annotations, raising an open question about what the model actually learns.** As described in Section 3.2, GPT-4V is fed verified bounding boxes, captions, and CoT exemplars—it does not discover evidence from pixels. The training data consists of *post-hoc* rationales of a reasoning process that was never actually executed from pixels. The concern is that the SFT stage teaches the model the *syntax* of grounded reasoning (the Planning–Grounding–Synthesis structure) without necessarily teaching the *perceptual skill* of deriving spatial evidence from visual features. The GRPO stage and zero-shot results partially mitigate this, but the paper provides no analysis to distinguish genuine pixel-level reasoning from stylistic mimicry. Notably, the failure analysis (Figure 7) cuts both ways: the model produces structurally perfect but factually wrong reasoning—exactly what one would expect from stylistic template matching. The paper acknowledges this limitation in the Conclusion but does not provide any probing evidence to address it.

2. **The claimed importance of GRPO is not well-supported by the evidence.** The paper states that the two-stage strategy "is *essential* for faithfully eliciting this capability" (Contributions, Section 1). However, Table 8 shows that GRPO provides only modest gains over SFT (w/ CoT) alone: VG mIoU +1.32, Counting MAE -0.15, Detection mAP@0.5 +3.03, VQA Acc +3.04. By contrast, the SFT stage with structured CoT rationales does the overwhelming majority of the work (e.g., Detection mAP@0.5 jumps from 3.56 to 74.03 with SFT+CoT from the base model—a +70.47 gain). Furthermore, applying GRPO without CoT rationales (SFT w/o CoT + GRPO) can harm performance (Counting MAE 4.51 vs. 2.93 for SFT w/ CoT alone). The paper would be stronger by honestly framing GRPO as a marginal refinement tool rather than an essential component, which is consistent with the evidence.

### Minor

1. **"First" claims are imprecisely stated.** The abstract calls Geo-CoT380k "the first large-scale dataset of structured Geo-CoT rationales" and Figure 1 calls RSThinker the "First VLM for Geospatial Reasoning." Yet Section 2.3 cites prior works (SegEarth-R1, RemoteReasoner, SkySense-O) that generate step-by-step rationales for remote sensing. While the paper correctly argues these lack spatial grounding, the "first" framing invites unnecessary skepticism. Qualifying with "perceptually-grounded" or "spatially-verifiable" would be more accurate and better reflect the actual contribution.

2. **Inconsistency between stated principle and the qualitative example.** Section 1 claims Geo-CoT produces "assertions explicitly linked to specific spatial references," but the reasoning trace in Figure 5 (lines 305–308) uses only textual descriptions ("terminal area at the center," "one side," "opposite side") without any bounding box coordinates. This creates a coherence gap between the framework's definition and its instantiation. Either the framework permits non-coordinate spatial references (in which case the definition should clarify this), or the model's outputs should include explicit coordinates to match the stated principle.

3. **No variance or significance reporting.** All results in Tables 4–8 are point estimates without standard deviations, confidence intervals, or multiple-seed runs. Given the number of comparisons and the modest GRPO margins, some measure of variance would increase confidence that the reported gains are reliable.

4. **One notable underperformance is not discussed.** In Table 7 (Image Captioning), RSThinker's NWPU-Captions CIDEr (94.81) is notably lower than EarthDial (123.6). The paper does not comment on this. Providing a hypothesis (e.g., the structured CoT format may sometimes harm caption fluency) would be helpful.

5. **The KL divergence ablation (Figure 4) only covers 1000 steps**, making it unclear whether the observed stabilization pattern generalizes to the full training run.

### Trivial
None.

## Nice-to-Haves

- A control experiment generating CoT rationales with a cheaper method (templates or a weaker VLM) to test whether the *specific content* of GPT-4V rationales matters or whether the *structure* alone accounts for the SFT gains. This would directly address the concern about learning substance vs. style.
- An intervention experiment corrupting intermediate grounding steps in the CoT trace to check whether the final answer changes, testing whether the CoT trace is causal or epiphenomenal.
- Reporting of variance across seeds for at least the ablation experiments.

## Novel Insights

The review surfaces a productive tension: the paper's strongest evidence (large, consistent margins across diverse tasks including zero-shot) and its most significant methodological concern (data generation conditioned on privileged ground-truth annotations, raising the possibility of stylistic mimicry) pull in opposite directions. The failure analysis (Figure 7) crystallizes this tension—it simultaneously demonstrates the framework's auditability virtue and exemplifies the mimicry risk. Future work could resolve this by testing whether the model's intermediate grounding steps causally influence its final output (e.g., via intervention experiments) rather than only confirming that the output format matches the template.

## Suggestions

1. **Qualify the "first" claims** — e.g., "first large-scale dataset of *perceptually-grounded* Geo-CoT rationales" and "first VLM for *spatially-verifiable* geospatial reasoning." This aligns the framing with the actual related work the paper itself cites.

2. **Recalibrate the GRPO framing** — Acknowledge that SFT with structured CoT rationales is the primary driver of improvement and that GRPO provides measurable but marginal refinement. The phrase "essential for faithfully eliciting this capability" in the contributions should be softened.

3. **Clarify the grounding requirement** — Either (a) note that textual spatial references ("terminal area at the center") satisfy the "spatial reference" requirement, or (b) update the model's outputs in Figure 5 to include explicit coordinate references if that is the standard the framework demands.

4. **Add variance reporting** — For at least the ablation experiments in Table 8, report results across multiple runs or seeds.
