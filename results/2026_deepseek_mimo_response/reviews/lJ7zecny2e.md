## Summary
This paper introduces RSThinker, a Vision-Language Model for remote sensing that implements a Perceptually-Grounded Geospatial Chain-of-Thought (Geo-CoT) through a structured Planning → Grounding → Synthesis protocol. The authors construct Geo-CoT380k (~385K structured rationales generated by GPT-4V conditioned on ground-truth annotations) and train RSThinker via two-stage alignment: SFT followed by GRPO with task-specific reward functions. RSThinker reports large margins over existing VLMs across visual grounding, detection, counting, classification, captioning, and VQA tasks.

## Strengths
- **Comprehensive ablation cleanly isolating component contributions (Table 8):** Systematically varies CoT presence and GRPO across six tasks. CoT-SFT consistently outperforms non-CoT-SFT (VG mIoU 87.70 vs 81.80; Det mAP 74.03 vs 49.36), and GRPO without CoT rationales lags behind CoT-SFT alone (VG 86.47 vs 87.70), confirming the cognitive scaffold must precede RL refinement.
- **Large, consistent margins on fine-grained perception tasks:** On VRSBench-VG, RSThinker achieves 90.4 mIoU@0.5 vs. next-best GLM-4.1V-Thinking at 63.8 (Table 4). On HRRSD counting, 85.26 Acc vs. 61.48 for EarthDial (Table 5). Margins are substantial across multiple benchmarks and comparison sets.
- **First large-scale structured rationale dataset for RS (Geo-CoT380k):** 384,591 rationales across 6 task types from 10+ public benchmarks. The pipeline conditions GPT-4V on verified ground-truth annotations, a principled approach to generating high-fidelity training data.
- **KL regularization analysis demonstrating practical training insight (Figure 4):** Concrete evidence that removing KL during GRPO causes "format reward collapse" after ~200 steps. Valuable for the community applying GRPO to structured reasoning tasks.
- **Honest failure analysis demonstrating the verifiability advantage (Figure 7):** The failure case where a dock extension is misidentified as a ship shows the explicit grounding mechanism exposing the error via bounding box [413, 225], making the hallucination immediately auditable.

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None

### Major
- **Missing experimental comparisons with the most directly related prior work (Section 2.3):** The paper discusses SegEarth-R1, RemoteReasoner, SkySense-O, and Ringmo-Agent as prior RS reasoning systems and devotes considerable text to arguing their inferiority. Yet none appear in any experimental table (Tables 4–8). These are the paper's closest competitors, and the "state-of-the-art" and "first" claims rest on comparisons with generalist VLMs and domain-specific VLMs that were not designed for structured reasoning. The authors argue these lack "perceptual grounding" and a "methodical cognitive architecture" (Section 2.3), but this is demonstrated only through argumentation, not experiment. Adding comparisons with even one or two of these systems on shared benchmarks would significantly strengthen the SOTA claims.
- **GRPO uses task-specific evaluation metrics as rewards, creating a systematic evaluation confound:** Table 3 shows GRPO directly optimizes IoU for grounding, mAP for detection, MAE for counting, and BLEU/METEOR/CIDEr for captioning — exactly the metrics used for evaluation. Many baselines received no such optimization. The paper does not discuss this confound. While the ablation (Table 8) shows CoT-SFT helps independently of GRPO, the specific magnitude of GRPO improvement cannot be cleanly attributed to the Geo-CoT reasoning structure vs. direct metric optimization. An ablation comparing GRPO with task-specific vs. generic rewards would disentangle these contributions.

### Minor
- **"Verifiability" claims are not quantitatively validated:** The central thesis is that Geo-CoT produces "verifiable" reasoning linked to "specific spatial references" and "specific pixel regions" (Abstract, Sections 1, 3). However, the qualitative example in Figure 5 shows only descriptive text ("three on one side of the terminal, two on the other side") without explicit spatial coordinates in the reasoning trace. While Figure 7 shows a bounding box in a failure case, the degree to which the deployed model's reasoning traces contain verifiable coordinate-grounded references vs. vague descriptive text is never measured. A simple quantitative analysis would substantiate or qualify this core distinguishing claim.
- **Unacknowledged mixed results on some benchmarks:** On VRSBench-VQA "Quantity" (Table 6), RSThinker scores 56.67 vs. Kimi-VL-Thinking's 74.67. On NWPU-Captions CIDEr (Table 7), EarthDial scores 123.6 vs. RSThinker's 94.81. These are not discussed despite the narrative of universal dominance. Acknowledging where the approach falls short and analyzing why would increase credibility.

None

## Removed Points
These points are flagged to be removed; treat them with caution:
- **Reviewer concern about GRPO training data overlapping with evaluation benchmarks** — The datasets in Table 2 (RSVQA-HR-train, NWPU-Captions-train, etc.) are explicitly training splits; standard train/test splits are used. This is common practice and not a real issue.
- **Reviewer concern about lack of variance reporting** — While nice-to-have, large-scale VLM benchmarks typically report single-run results. This is field-standard and does not constitute a real weakness.

## Novel Insights
The paper's most genuinely novel insight is that the structured Geo-CoT cognitive scaffold (SFT with CoT) is a necessary prerequisite for effective RL-based refinement — GRPO without the CoT scaffold fails to recover the same performance (Table 8, SFT w/o CoT + GRPO vs. SFT w/ CoT). This symbiotic relationship between cognitive structure and outcome-based optimization is a meaningful finding for the broader reasoning-in-VLMs community, extending beyond the remote sensing domain.

## Suggestions
1. Add experimental comparisons with SegEarth-R1 and/or RemoteReasoner on shared benchmarks to ground the SOTA claims — this is the single highest-leverage improvement.
2. Add an ablation comparing GRPO with task-specific vs. generic rewards to disentangle metric optimization from reasoning architecture contributions.
3. Quantify the degree of spatial grounding in RSThinker's reasoning traces (e.g., % of steps containing explicit coordinate references vs. descriptive text).
4. Acknowledge and analyze the mixed results on quantity VQA and NWPU-Captions CIDEr.
