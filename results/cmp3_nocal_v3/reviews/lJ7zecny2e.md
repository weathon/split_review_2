## Summary

This paper introduces the Perceptually-Grounded Geospatial Chain-of-Thought (Geo-CoT) framework for remote sensing VLMs, alongside Geo-CoT380k (a dataset of 384k structured rationales) and RSThinker (a model trained via SFT + GRPO). The key idea is to replace opaque end-to-end VLM inference with a verifiable, multi-step reasoning process where each claim is explicitly grounded in visual evidence. The paper evaluates across a broad suite of RS benchmarks and reports large performance margins over existing models.

## Strengths

- **Well-motivated problem with a clear diagnosis (Section 1, Section 2.2).** The paper correctly identifies that existing RS VLMs produce answers without verifiable reasoning traces, and makes a genuine distinction between "semantic interpretation" (relying on parametric knowledge) and "visual investigation" (grounding claims in spatial evidence). This is a real gap.

- **Large-scale dataset contribution (Geo-CoT380k, Table 1).** 384,591 structured rationales across VQA, captioning, classification, grounding, counting, and detection. The pipeline of retrofitting ground-truth bounding boxes with GPT-4V-generated rationales conditioned on verified annotations is practical and scalable. If released, this would be a useful community resource.

- **Broad evaluation (Tables 4–7).** Evaluation across 6 task categories and 15+ benchmarks, including zero-shot settings, is more comprehensive than typical for RS VLM papers.

- **Honest failure analysis (Figure 7).** The paper shows a concrete failure case where the model produces a structurally sound but factually wrong reasoning chain, and correctly notes that the explicit grounding *exposes* the error — turning a silent hallucination into an auditable one. This is intellectually honest.

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None.

### Major

1. **Baseline comparison confounds method advantage with dataset-specific fine-tuning.** RSThinker is fine-tuned on the training splits of benchmarks it is evaluated on (VRSBench-train-VG → VRSBench-VG; DIOR-RSVG-train → DIOR-RSVG; DOTAv2-train → DOTAv2-val; HRRSD-train → HRRSD; NWPU-RESISC45-train → RESISC45; AID-train → AID; etc. — see Table 1). The baselines — including GeoChat, EarthDial, VHM, SkySenseGPT, and GLM-4.1V-Thinking — are evaluated off-the-shelf without comparable in-distribution fine-tuning. The reported margins are unusually large (e.g., DIOR-RSVG @0.5: 93.1 vs. 59.6; HRRSD counting MAE: 0.242 vs. 0.871; RSOD counting ZS Acc: 95.5 vs. 51.5). Training on the same data and testing on held-out splits is standard practice; the issue is that the baselines did **not** receive equivalent training on those splits, so the comparison conflates the benefit of the Geo-CoT methodology with the benefit of simply being fine-tuned on the evaluation domain. The zero-shot columns (RRSIS-D ZS, RSVG ZS, RSOD ZS, NWPU-VHR ZS, RS19 ZS, SIRI ZS, UCM ZS) are cleaner but still show large gaps that partly reflect RSThinker's stronger base model (GLM-4.1V-9B) vs. smaller backbones in some baselines. The paper should either (a) fine-tune leading baselines on the same Geo-CoT380k data and compare fairly, or (b) restructure the headline results to exclusively use zero-shot or held-out benchmarks, explicitly acknowledging which evaluations benefit from in-distribution training.

### Minor

2. **The claimed necessity of the two-stage process is overstated relative to the ablation evidence.** Contribution 3 (page 2) states that "a two-stage alignment strategy of SFT as a prerequisite for reinforcement learning (GRPO) is essential." Table 8 shows that **SFT (w/ CoT) alone** — without GRPO — achieves the vast majority of the gains over the base model (e.g., VG mIoU: 87.70 vs. full pipeline's 89.02; Detection mAP@0.5: 74.03 vs. 77.06; Counting MAE: 2.93 vs. 2.78). The GRPO improvements are consistent but modest (1–4% relative on most metrics). Additionally, the ablation is missing a "Base + GRPO only" condition (no SFT at all), which would be needed to fully support the claim that SFT is a "prerequisite." The paper should soften the "essential" framing, report the missing ablation condition, and more honestly characterize the magnitude of GRPO's contribution.

3. **No quality verification of the GPT-4V-generated rationales.** The entire approach depends on Geo-CoT380k's 384k rationales, generated by GPT-4V conditioned on ground-truth annotations. The paper states this pipeline "minimizes the risk of hallucinated reasoning" (Section 3.2) but provides no quantitative quality assessment — no human evaluation, no agreement rates, no analysis of how often GPT-4V deviates from provided ground-truth boxes, no comparison to human-written rationales. The failure case (Figure 7) shows exactly the kind of error that could originate in the training data: a structurally fluent but factually wrong trace. Without knowing the quality of the training rationales, it is difficult to assess whether the framework teaches genuine grounded reasoning or stylistic mimicry. A human evaluation of 200–500 samples would substantially strengthen this contribution.

4. **No controlled comparison for base model capacity.** RSThinker uses GLM-4.1V-9B-Base with Aimv2-Huge, a strong backbone. The paper does not control for base model size/capacity when making performance comparisons. Some baselines (e.g., EarthDial) likely use smaller backbones, making it unclear how much of the reported gain is due to the Geo-CoT framework vs. the underlying model's capacity.

### Trivial

5. **The qualitative grounding example (Figure 5) uses relative spatial language rather than explicit coordinates in the reasoning trace.** The trace says "three on one side of the terminal, two on the opposite side" — these are spatial references but not explicit, falsifiable coordinates. The paper's definition of perceptual grounding requires "assertions explicitly linked to specific spatial references" (Section 1). The distinction should be clarified: whether the grounding occurs at the level of textual spatial reasoning or requires explicit coordinate output in the trace.

## Suggestions

1. **Relevel the baseline comparisons:** Fine-tune the strongest open-source baselines (GLM-4.1V-Thinking, Qwen2.5-VL) on the Geo-CoT380k data (or a matched subset) with equivalent compute and report the results alongside the current comparisons. Alternatively, restructure the paper so that zero-shot evaluations are the primary evidence, with in-distribution results clearly caveated.
2. **Validate rationale quality:** Conduct a human evaluation of 200–500 GPT-4V-generated rationales, measuring factual correctness relative to ground-truth boxes and the presence of genuine perceptual grounding (spatial references).
3. **Add the missing ablation condition:** Report "Base + GRPO only" in Table 8 to directly test whether SFT is truly a prerequisite.
4. **Soften the "essential" claim** about the two-stage process to reflect the magnitude of GRPO's actual contribution.
5. **Clarify the grounding format:** Specify whether the model is expected to output explicit coordinates in its reasoning trace or whether textual spatial references suffice.
