## Summary

This paper introduces RSThinker, a vision-language model for remote sensing that generates Perceptually-Grounded Geospatial Chain-of-Thought (Geo-CoT) reasoning. The core contributions are: (1) Geo-CoT380k, a 384k-sample SFT dataset of structured rationales generated by conditioning GPT-4V on verified bounding boxes; (2) a two-stage training pipeline (SFT then GRPO); and (3) comprehensive multi-task evaluation showing substantial gains over zero-shot baselines, with particularly strong zero-shot generalization on grounding benchmarks (RRSIS-D: 94.0% vs. 72.5% for EarthDial).

## Strengths

- **Honest ablation design (Table 8).** The paper correctly structures ablations along the axes that matter — base → SFT w/o CoT → SFT w/ CoT → each plus GRPO — allowing separate attribution of gains to domain fine-tuning, CoT structure, and RL. This is more rigorous than many domain-adaptation papers.

- **Strong zero-shot generalization on grounding (Table 4).** On RRSIS-D and RSVG, which RSThinker was *not* trained on, it achieves 94.0% @0.5 vs. 72.5% (EarthDial) and 64.0% vs. 43.0% (GLM-4.1V-Thinking) respectively. These results provide the cleanest test of whether Geo-CoT generalizes beyond in-distribution training data.

- **KL regularization analysis (Figure 4).** The "format reward collapse" visualization provides concrete evidence for a design decision, clearly demonstrating the instability that arises when KL regularization is removed from GRPO.

- **Honest failure mode presentation (Figure 7).** The paper shows a case where a structurally sound reasoning trace misidentifies a dock as a ship, and correctly argues that externalized failure is more useful than opaque wrong answers — a fair and specific claim.

- **Symbiotic SFT-RL finding (Table 8).** GRPO applied on top of SFT w/o CoT substantially underperforms SFT w/ CoT without GRPO on most tasks (e.g., 56.77 vs. 74.03 mAP), revealing that the Geo-CoT cognitive scaffold must exist before outcome-based RL can meaningfully refine it. This is a concrete and actionable empirical insight.

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None.

### Major

- **Main results conflate domain adaptation with architectural contribution.** Table 8 reveals the relative contributions: SFT w/o CoT over base yields +25.54 mIoU, +45.80 mAP, +55.41 VQA accuracy. Adding CoT to SFT contributes an incremental +5.90 mIoU, +24.67 mAP, +10.63 VQA. Domain fine-tuning is the primary driver. However, Tables 4–7 compare RSThinker only against zero-shot baselines, and the paper attributes the full performance gap to "a fundamental architectural divergence" (Section 4.2.1) and "a direct consequence of the Geo-CoT architecture." The "SFT w/o CoT" baseline — which would much more closely approximate an equivalently domain-adapted non-CoT model — never appears in the main results tables. A reader consulting only Tables 4–7 would conclude the gap is architectural; Table 8 reveals it is primarily due to domain fine-tuning. The overclaiming in the main text is significant and the framing should be corrected.

- **"Verifiable reasoning" is evaluated only through final-answer metrics.** The paper's foundational claim is verifiability: "a verifiable link between each analytical step and its corresponding visual evidence" (Introduction, Section 3). Yet all quantitative evaluation uses final-answer metrics (mIoU, mAP, Accuracy, BLEU). There is no measurement of reasoning faithfulness — whether cited bounding boxes correspond to real objects, whether reasoning steps are logically consistent with final answers, or whether CoT traces contain meaningful spatial grounding versus stylistic text. Figure 5's reasoning trace notably uses verbal groupings only ("three airplanes on one side of the terminal") without coordinate-level grounding within the trace, while Figure 7's failure case does include a specific coordinate ([413, 225]). This inconsistency makes it unclear whether coordinate grounding in reasoning traces is systematic. If verifiability is the paper's founding motivation, at minimum a small-scale human evaluation or coordinate-level analysis is needed.

### Minor

- **VQA Quantity subcategory (Table 6) unexplained.** RSThinker achieves 56.67 on VQA Quantity, substantially below Gemini-2.0-flash (86.00). This is notable because Geo-CoT's Planning–Grounding–Synthesize mechanism is positioned as specifically advantageous for counting tasks; the weak performance on quantity-type VQA questions is unaddressed and partially undercuts this claim.

- **NWPU-Captions CIDEr gap (Table 7) unexplained.** EarthDial outperforms RSThinker on NWPU-Captions CIDEr (123.6 vs. 94.81). The paper acknowledges this via formatting but provides no discussion of why — whether this reflects a training data difference, a limitation of the CoT approach for dense captioning, or another factor.

### Trivial

- Equations 1–2 (Section 3.1) describe the bicubic positional encoding interpolation of the inherited GLM-4.1V-Base backbone. Including them in the method section without labeling them as an inherited property slightly inflates apparent architectural novelty.

## Nice-to-Haves

- Move the "SFT w/o CoT" model into the main comparison tables (Tables 4–7) as a primary reference baseline. This would make the architectural vs. adaptation distinction transparent and would actually *strengthen* the paper's case by showing the incremental CoT gain more clearly.
- Add a lightweight evaluation of reasoning trace faithfulness: e.g., for a sample of outputs, annotate whether coordinate references in reasoning traces correspond to real objects, and whether the stated reasoning is logically consistent with the final answer.
- Clarify whether bounding box coordinates appear systematically in Geo-CoT reasoning traces or only selectively; quantify the proportion of traces containing spatial coordinate references.
- Specify or briefly note how the captioning reward weights (W_m in Table 3) were selected.

## Removed Points

*These points are flagged to be removed; treat them with caution.*

- **Introduction lacks systematic survey of prior work framing:** Reviewer flagged that "prevailing approaches often frame reasoning as a process of semantic interpretation rather than visual investigation" is stated without survey. However, Section 2.3 cites and explicitly characterizes several specific works (SegEarth-R1, RemoteReasoner, SkySense-O, Ringmo-Agent) with their limitations. The claim is adequately grounded for an introduction. Removed.

- **Double-counting in Table 1:** DOTAv2-train and HRRSD-train each appear in both Object Counting and Object Detection rows. This is standard multi-task dataset construction where the same images generate separate task-specific CoT rationale entries. The total 384,591 refers to distinct rationale instances, not necessarily unique images. Not deceptive; removed.

- **Section 4.4 church example "overstated":** Reviewer argues that identifying a church from domes and red roof is "semantic pattern matching." This is partially fair, but the paper does frame the capability correctly as "systematic search for specific architectural affordances." The criticism is too speculative to retain. Removed.

## Novel Insights

The paper's most insightful empirical finding — partially buried in Table 8 — is the asymmetric interdependence of SFT and GRPO: GRPO applied to an SFT-without-CoT checkpoint cannot recover the performance achieved by SFT-with-CoT alone, while GRPO applied to an SFT-with-CoT checkpoint provides further consistent gains. This suggests that outcome-based RL refinement requires a pre-existing cognitive scaffold, not just task-relevant fine-tuning, to be effective. This finding has implications for how the community orders and combines SFT and RL stages in domain-specialized reasoning VLMs.

## Suggestions

1. Restructure the main results tables to include SFT w/o CoT as a primary comparison baseline, making the "CoT vs. no CoT at matched scale" comparison the paper's central evidence rather than a cross-referenced ablation.
2. Address the VQA Quantity subcategory gap (56.67 vs. 86.00 for Gemini) explicitly, either with additional analysis or a revised claim about counting capability.
3. Revise language in Section 4.2.1 from "a direct consequence of the Geo-CoT architecture" and "fundamental architectural divergence" to language more precisely reflecting that both domain fine-tuning and the CoT structure contribute, with CoT providing meaningful incremental gains.
4. Add at least a qualitative systematic analysis of whether coordinate grounding in reasoning traces is consistent across outputs, even if a full human evaluation is deferred.
