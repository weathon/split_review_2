## Summary
This paper proposes the Semantic Disentanglement Error (also named Semantic Separability Error and Semantic Decomposition Error, depending on the section), a metric measuring linear recoverability of trend vs. seasonal components from a shared embedding, and an Asymmetric Perceptual Weighting (APW) scheme that plugs into the CoST contrastive framework by dynamically re-weighting its trend and seasonal losses using an asymmetry factor Δ. A synthetic experiment on TS2Vec demonstrates the existence of semantic imbalance, and Table 3 compares CoST+APW against TS2Vec, CoST, and TNC on ETT, Electricity, and Weather forecasting benchmarks.

## Strengths
- **Concrete motivating experiment (Table 1).** The synthetic SDE analysis cleanly demonstrates that TS2Vec representations are asymmetrically biased as a function of component amplitude ratio r. At r=1 values are approximately symmetric (SDE_trend,period=0.438, SDE_period,trend=0.411); at extremes the imbalance is large. This is a well-structured diagnostic finding that legitimately motivates the research direction.
- **Plug-in design.** Requiring no architectural changes to CoST beyond an MLP fusion head and re-weighted losses lowers the barrier to adoption if the method were validated.

## Weaknesses

### Fatal

- **Central claim is directly refuted by the paper's own Table 3.** The abstract states "consistent gains in forecasting accuracy," and Section 4.4.4/Conclusion state "consistently lower SDE values and superior forecasting accuracy." Table 3 contradicts both. On Electricity at horizons 168, 336, and 720, CoST+APW records MSE of 0.566, 0.677, and 1.010, versus CoST's 0.425, 0.576, and 0.911, and TS2Vec's 0.429, 0.565, and 0.863 — degradations of 17–33% at the longest horizons, where modeling seasonal patterns matters most. On Weather at horizons 336 and 720, CoST+APW (MSE 0.266, 0.299) loses to every baseline, including the weakest one (TNC: 0.215, 0.219). The empirical evidence does not support the paper's core claim.

- **SDE values promised in Table 3 are never reported.** Section 4.4.4 explicitly states "Table 3 reports the SDE metrics and forecasting performance." Table 3 contains only MSE and MAE columns — no SDE values appear at all. The paper's only SDE values come from a synthetic TS2Vec experiment (Table 1), not from any real dataset and not from the proposed CoST+APW method. The claim of "consistently lower SDE values" in the Conclusion is entirely unsubstantiated.

- **The paper's central contribution has three different names within seven pages.** The title uses "Semantic Disentanglement Error." Section 3.2 introduces it as "Semantic Separability Error." Section 4.3 and the metrics description in Section 4.1 call it "Semantic Decomposition Error." All three use the acronym SDE. A paper that proposes a named metric as its core diagnostic contribution cannot name that metric three different ways in the main body.

### Major

- **Structural incompatibility between Section 3 and Section 4 is never reconciled.** Section 3 defines SDE using a single shared encoder f_θ: v(a)=f_θ(a), v(b)=f_θ(b), v(a+b)=f_θ(x). Section 4.4 redefines the setup around CoST's dual encoders, where v(a)=pool(V_T(x)) and v(b)=pool(V_S(x)), and v(a+b) is produced by an entirely new MLP g_φ applied to the concatenation. These are structurally different architectures. The SDE defined in Section 3 does not straightforwardly transfer to the Section 4 setup, and the paper provides no derivation bridging the two formulations.

- **Dataset labeling error in Table 3.** Table 3 contains two sections both labeled "ETT1." The second one (rows with horizons {24, 48, 96, 288, 672}) corresponds to the minute-resolution ETTm1 dataset, not hourly ETT1. This mislabeling means readers cannot determine which results correspond to which dataset.

### Minor

- **Ablation does not isolate the MLP fusion head.** The added MLP g_φ contributes additional parameters and expressivity independent of APW. No experiment separates the contribution of MLP capacity from the asymmetric weighting, making it impossible to attribute gains on the correctly-labeled ETTm1 rows to the reweighting mechanism rather than the added parameters.

- **Hyperparameters γ and γ' never reported.** Introduced in Sections 3.4 and 4.4.3, their values are nowhere in the paper, preventing reproducibility.

### Trivial

- **SDE values in Table 1 imply negative cosine similarity (e.g., SDE=1.328 → cos=−0.328) without comment.** This represents a more extreme failure mode than "low recoverability" and warrants a sentence of discussion.

## Suggestions

2. Actually compute and report SDE on real datasets for all methods in Table 3 — this is the paper's stated primary validation.
3. Fix the ETT1/ETTm1 mislabeling in Table 3.
4. Either revise the abstract and conclusion to accurately characterize the mixed results, or identify and fix the source of the severe degradation on Electricity at long horizons — the method's worst failures happen precisely where periodic modeling should help most.
5. Add a CoST + MLP-only (no APW) ablation row.
6. Report γ and γ' values.
