## Summary
TokenCount is a training-free object-counting pipeline built on SAM that (i) iteratively samples point prompts from a similarity-derived probability distribution over the image embedding and (ii) verifies candidate masks by comparing SAM decoder output tokens to exemplar tokens using TS-SS hybrid similarity. It is evaluated on FSC-147 and CARPK.

## Strengths
- Using SAM's decoder output tokens (rather than image embeddings) as the verification substrate is a well-motivated idea. Table 5 (16.25 vs 16.94 MAE) and the PCA visualization in Figure 5 give direct evidence that output tokens separate positive/negative samples better than image embeddings.
- Strong CARPK result: MAE 4.68 / RMSE 6.13 beats all listed training-based methods (BMNet+ 5.76/7.83, SAFECOUNT 5.33/7.04, LOCA 9.97/12.51) and all training-free SAM-only baselines.
- Iteration/prompt ablation (Table 4) provides a clear accuracy–efficiency trade-off curve, with a concrete early-stopping criterion (IoU>0.95, patience 5).

## Weaknesses

### Fatal
None.

### Major
- **Overclaimed FSC-147 performance.** The abstract states the method achieves "an MAE of 16.25, outperforming existing training-based and training-free counting methods", but Table 1 lists LOCA (10.79), PseCo (13.05), SAFECOUNT (14.32), BMNet+ (14.62), and the SAM+α training-free TFCAC (12.26) all with better MAE. The honest claim — "best among SAM-only training-free methods on FSC-147" — is supported; the broader claim is contradicted by the paper's own headline table.
- **RMSE 133.11 on FSC-147 is essentially the worst in Table 1, yet bolded as if it were a win and never analyzed.** Compared to LOCA's 56.97 and TFCAC's 56.33, this implies a heavy tail of catastrophic per-image errors. §5 alludes to failure on dense small instances but does not connect this to the RMSE or characterize where these failures occur.
- **TS-SS ablation does not support its theoretical motivation.** §3.2 argues at length that dot product, cosine, and L2 are each fundamentally inadequate because tokens entangle semantic and positional information. But Table 3 shows cosine 16.48, L2 16.53, TS-SS 16.25 — a 0.23 MAE gap with no variance reported. The §4.4 claim that "non-hybrid metrics have reached a performance plateau" is much stronger than the data warrants.

### Minor
- **Efficiency framing is asserted rather than measured.** The headline 16.25 MAE configuration uses 16×128 prompts (mean 1438 prompts/image at 1.69s/image). The "more than twice as fast" claim vs TFCAC and the comparative efficiency claims vs OmniCount/TFCounter are not supported by a wall-clock comparison table on matched hardware.
- **Two dataset-tuned scalars in a "training-free" pipeline.** τ=0.066 and verification threshold=300 are "set based on validation experiments" (§4.1). No sensitivity analysis or cross-dataset transfer is reported, which weakens the generality narrative.
- **Probability update rule (§3.1 / Figure 3) is informal.** The decrement rule for negatively-verified masks is described in prose ("the value of the probability distribution at that mask region is decreased") without an equation, hurting reproducibility of a core mechanism.
- **Quantitative support for the output-token claim rests on one ablation (Table 5, 0.69 MAE gap) and one two-example PCA figure.** A category-level separability analysis (e.g., silhouette, linear-probe accuracy across categories) would turn Figure 5 from suggestive into evidential.

### Trivial
- Angle-unit convention in Eq. 2 is mixed: θ/360° in SS implies degrees, while sin(θ) in TS needs the convention stated explicitly.

## Nice-to-Haves
- Failure-mode breakdown linking the high FSC-147 RMSE to image properties (density, scale, category).
- Variance across multiple random exemplar choices, particularly on CARPK where 12 exemplars are randomly drawn.
- Reposition the contribution around the CARPK result and the SAM-only training-free niche, which is where the evidence is genuinely strong.

## Suggestions
- Reframe FSC-147 result as "competitive among SAM-only training-free methods" rather than overall SOTA.
- Diagnose the FSC-147 RMSE tail with a per-image error analysis.
- Either find a regime where TS-SS clearly separates from cosine/L2, or report it as a small engineering choice and drop the strong theoretical framing.
- Add a wall-clock comparison table against TFCAC/OmniCount/TFCounter and a sensitivity analysis for τ and the verification threshold.
