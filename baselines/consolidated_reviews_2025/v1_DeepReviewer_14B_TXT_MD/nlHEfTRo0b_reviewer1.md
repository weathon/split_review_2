### Summary

The paper introduces a framework for learning the solution operator of parametric PDEs. The framework encodes the initial condition into a latent vector that is then integrated over time using a neural ODE. The latent dynamics is trained to minimize the physics-informed loss. The paper also introduces two regularization terms to stabilize the training process. The proposed approach is validated on two benchmark problems.
