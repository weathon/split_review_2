### Summary

The paper proposes a concept-based explainable image representation learning method to improve the interpretability of self-supervised learning representations. The method first uses a concept-based model with pretrained CLIP and concepts generated by GPT-4 to project input images into a concept vector space. Then, a Variational Autoencoder learns latent representations from these projected concepts, which serves as the final image representation. The method allows for attributions to a human-comprehensible concept space, enhancing interpretability while preserving robustness for downstream tasks.
