### Summary

The paper proposes a simple feed-forward NN model of a robotic arm, which is trained to reach to targets in a physics simulation. The paper proposes three different initial conditions or training methods, which are referred to as hypotheses H0, H1, and H2. H0 is a direct training of reaching to targets. H1 is a pre-training with a self-supervised learning with a differentiator model (although it is not called this way in the paper). H2 is a combination of H0 and H1. The paper then considers three different experiments, in which the reaching task is perturbed in three different ways. The paper reports that H2 outperforms H0 and H1 in all three experiments.
