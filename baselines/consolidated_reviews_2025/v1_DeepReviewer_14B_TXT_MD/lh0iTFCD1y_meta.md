## Summary

This paper introduces LUMA, a novel multimodal dataset designed to facilitate research in uncertainty quantification for deep learning models. The dataset combines image, audio, and text modalities, extending the well-known CIFAR-10/100 dataset with corresponding audio recordings and text descriptions generated using the Gemma-7B Large Language Model. A key contribution of LUMA is its ability to inject controlled levels of aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty, allowing researchers to systematically evaluate the robustness of multimodal models under various noise conditions. The authors provide a Python package that enables users to manipulate data diversity, noise levels for each modality, and the inclusion of out-of-distribution samples. Baseline models and three uncertainty quantification methods are also included, serving as a starting point for further research. The paper aims to address the limitations of existing datasets by providing a controlled environment for studying uncertainty in multimodal settings. The authors anticipate that LUMA will aid the research community in designing more robust and trustworthy machine learning approaches, particularly for safety-critical applications. The core methodology involves extending CIFAR-10/100 with audio pronunciations of class labels extracted from various corpora and generating text descriptions using a large language model. The dataset is designed to allow for the controlled injection of noise and uncertainty, which is achieved through various techniques such as reducing sample diversity and introducing label noise. The authors provide baseline experiments using a late fusion approach and three uncertainty quantification methods: Monte Carlo Dropout, Deep Ensembles, and Reliable Conflictive Multi-View Learning. These experiments demonstrate the dataset's utility for evaluating model performance under different noise conditions. The overall significance of this work lies in the creation of a multimodal dataset with controlled uncertainty, which is a valuable resource for the research community working on trustworthy multimodal deep learning. The dataset's extensibility and the provided Python package further enhance its utility for benchmarking and developing robust multimodal models. However, as I will detail in the weaknesses section, there are several areas where the paper could be improved to fully realize the potential of the LUMA dataset.
