### Summary

The paper introduces Delta, an inference-time approach designed to reduce hallucinations in large language models (LLMs) without the need for retraining or additional training data. Delta leverages contrastive decoding by masking portions of the input prompt and contrasting the original and masked output distributions generated by the model. This method effectively reduces hallucinations through inference-only computations. The paper evaluates Delta on context-rich QA benchmarks like SQuAD v1.1 and v2, showing significant improvements. However, it notes that Delta is less effective on tasks without explicit contextual information, such as CommonsenseQA and MMLU.
