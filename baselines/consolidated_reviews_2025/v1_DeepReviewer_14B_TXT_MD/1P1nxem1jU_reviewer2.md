### Summary

The paper proposes a new graph data augmentation method for graph classification tasks. The method is based on the observation that the low-frequency eigenvalues of the graph Laplacian are closely related to the graph properties. The proposed method, called Dual-Prism (DP), consists of two steps: 1) decomposing the graph Laplacian into its eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and 2) modifying the eigenvalues by adding noise or masking them. The modified eigenvalues are then used to reconstruct the augmented graph. The paper evaluates the proposed method on 21 real-world datasets across three different domains, using four different learning settings. The results show that the proposed method outperforms the baselines in most cases, and that it can preserve the graph properties while enhancing the diversity of the augmented graphs.
