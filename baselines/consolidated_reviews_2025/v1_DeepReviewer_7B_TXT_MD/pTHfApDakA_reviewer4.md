### Summary

This paper proposes a zero-shot method to improve the reasoning ability of LLMs by self-checking their reasoning steps. The method consists of four stages: target extraction, information collection, step regeneration, and result integration. The authors conduct experiments on three datasets and show that the proposed method can improve the final answer accuracies of LLMs.
