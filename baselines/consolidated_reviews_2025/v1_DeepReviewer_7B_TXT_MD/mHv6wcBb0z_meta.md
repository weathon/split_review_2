## Summary

This paper introduces a novel regularization method, termed NR-DCCA, designed to address the model collapse issue prevalent in deep canonical correlation analysis (DCCA) for multi-view representation learning (MVRL). The authors identify that the performance degradation of DCCA-based methods stems from the low-rank nature of the weight matrices used in deep neural networks, which leads to a collapse in the learned representations. To counter this, they propose the Correlation Invariant Property (CIP), which posits that the correlation between the original data and the noise should be invariant. This property, they argue, is equivalent to ensuring that the weight matrices are full-rank. The NR-DCCA method enforces this property by adding a noise regularization term to the DCCA objective function. The core idea is that by making the correlation invariant to noise, the method will be less prone to model collapse. The authors provide a theoretical analysis of the CIP, demonstrating its connection to the full-rank property and its impact on the representation and denoising loss. Empirically, the authors evaluate NR-DCCA on both synthetic and real-world datasets, showing that it outperforms existing DCCA-based methods in terms of stability and performance. The experiments include comparisons with various DCCA baselines on datasets with different numbers of views, demonstrating the robustness of the proposed method. The results suggest that NR-DCCA is a promising approach for mitigating model collapse in DCCA-based MVRL methods. The paper's contribution lies in the identification of the low-rank issue, the introduction of the CIP, and the development of a practical regularization method that effectively addresses the model collapse problem. The theoretical analysis provides a solid foundation for the proposed approach, and the empirical results validate its effectiveness. Overall, the paper presents a well-motivated and well-executed study that makes a significant contribution to the field of multi-view representation learning.
