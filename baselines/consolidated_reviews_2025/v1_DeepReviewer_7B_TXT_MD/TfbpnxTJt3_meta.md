## Summary

This paper introduces FedDPCont, a novel framework designed to address the challenge of learning with local openset noisy labels in federated learning (FL). The core problem tackled is the presence of label noise that varies across clients, a scenario not adequately addressed by existing FL methods that assume identical label spaces. The authors propose a method that generates openset labels via globally shared private contrastive labels to mitigate overfitting to noisy labels. To ensure privacy, the framework employs label differential privacy (DP), which is a key contribution. The authors provide theoretical guarantees for the proposed method, including convergence and robustness to label noise. Empirically, FedDPCont is evaluated on both synthetic and real-world datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in handling openset noisy labels. The paper's main contributions include the formalization of the openset noisy label problem in FL, the introduction of a label privacy mechanism, and the provision of theoretical guarantees. The experimental results show that FedDPCont outperforms several baseline methods on benchmark datasets like CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, as well as on real-world datasets like Clothing-1M and CIFAR-N. The authors also explore the impact of the privacy parameter epsilon on the performance of the model. Overall, this paper presents a significant step towards addressing the practical challenges of learning with noisy labels in federated settings, particularly when the noise characteristics vary across clients. The work is significant because it addresses a realistic problem in FL, where data is often heterogeneous and noisy. The proposed method offers a way to mitigate the impact of local noisy labels while preserving privacy. The theoretical analysis provides a solid foundation for the proposed method, and the empirical results demonstrate its effectiveness. However, the paper has some limitations, particularly in the areas of experimental details, comparisons with existing methods, and the discussion of limitations. These limitations should be addressed in future work to further enhance the impact of the paper.
