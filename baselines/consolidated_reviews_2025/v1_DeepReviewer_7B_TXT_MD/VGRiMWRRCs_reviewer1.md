### Summary

This paper proposes a new method for group robustness by estimating group information from model uncertainty. The authors formulate the problem in an Empirical Bayes framework and propose a method to estimate group probabilities from the model's epistemic uncertainty. The method is tested on Colored MNIST, Waterbirds, CelebA, MultiNLI, and CivilComments datasets, showing improved group robustness and reduced hyperparameter tuning requirements.
