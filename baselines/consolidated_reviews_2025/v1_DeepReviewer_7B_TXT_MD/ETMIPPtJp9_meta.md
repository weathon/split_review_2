## Summary

This paper introduces FiDeLiS, a novel retrieval-augmented reasoning method designed for knowledge graph question answering (KGQA). The core idea behind FiDeLiS is to enhance the reliability and accuracy of reasoning paths by integrating a keyword-enhanced retrieval mechanism (Path-RAG) with a deductive-verification beam search (DVBS). The Path-RAG component retrieves entities and relations from the knowledge graph that are relevant to a given query, using a keyword-enhanced approach to refine the retrieval process. This is followed by the DVBS, which generates reasoning paths by expanding the retrieved entities and relations using a beam search algorithm. A key aspect of DVBS is its deductive-verification step, which assesses the logical validity of each step in the reasoning path, ensuring that the generated paths are grounded in verifiable entities and relations within the knowledge graph. The authors evaluate FiDeLiS on three standard KGQA datasets: WebQSP, CWQ, and CR-LT, demonstrating that it outperforms several established baselines in terms of accuracy, generality, and computational efficiency. The experimental results show that FiDeLiS achieves higher accuracy, generality, and computational efficiency compared to existing methods. The ablation studies further validate the effectiveness of each component of the proposed method. The paper also includes a runtime comparison with baseline methods, showing that FiDeLiS is computationally efficient. Overall, the paper presents a well-structured and clearly written approach to KGQA, offering a promising solution for reliable and accurate reasoning over knowledge graphs. The integration of keyword-enhanced retrieval with a deductive-verification beam search is a significant contribution, addressing key challenges in KGQA, such as data sparsity and the complexity of query interpretation. The empirical results support the effectiveness of the proposed method, making it a valuable addition to the field of knowledge graph question answering.

## Weaknesses
