## Summary

This paper introduces AdvI2I, a novel adversarial image attack framework designed to manipulate image-to-image (I2I) diffusion models into generating NSFW content. The core idea revolves around extracting adversarial image vectors from the text embedding space, which are then used to guide the generation of NSFW content in images. The framework leverages an adversarial image generator to create perturbations that, when applied to benign images, induce the diffusion model to produce outputs containing NSFW content. The authors also propose an adaptive version of the framework, AdvI2I-Adaptive, which aims to enhance the robustness of the attack against existing defense mechanisms. The paper evaluates the effectiveness of both frameworks on two state-of-the-art I2I diffusion models, InstructPix2Pix and SDv1.5-Inpainting, demonstrating their ability to bypass current safeguards and achieve high attack success rates. The empirical findings highlight the vulnerability of these models to adversarial attacks and underscore the urgent need for stronger security measures in generative models. The authors present a comprehensive evaluation of their framework, including comparisons with existing methods and an analysis of its robustness against various defense mechanisms. The results demonstrate the potential of adversarial attacks to compromise the safety and integrity of I2I diffusion models, raising significant concerns about their responsible use. The paper's contribution lies in its introduction of a novel adversarial attack specifically tailored for I2I models, its detailed evaluation of the framework's performance, and its emphasis on the need for improved security measures in generative models. The work is significant because it addresses a growing concern in the field of generative AI, where the potential for misuse of these models to produce harmful content is a major ethical and societal issue. By demonstrating the vulnerability of I2I models to adversarial attacks, the paper calls for a more concerted effort to develop robust and secure generative models that can be used responsibly.
