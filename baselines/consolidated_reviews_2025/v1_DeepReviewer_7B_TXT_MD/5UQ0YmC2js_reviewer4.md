### Summary

This paper proposes a novel adversarial image attack framework, AdvI2I, to manipulate image-to-image (I2I) diffusion models to generate NSFW content. The framework extracts adversarial image vectors from the text embedding space to guide the generation of NSFW content in images. The authors also introduce an adaptive version of the framework, AdvI2I-Adaptive, which is designed to resist potential countermeasures and minimize the resemblance between the generated adversarial images and NSFW concept embeddings, making it harder for safety checkers to identify them as harmful content. The paper evaluates the effectiveness of both frameworks on two diffusion models, InstructPix2Pix and SDv1.5-Inpainting, and demonstrates their ability to bypass current safeguards, highlighting the urgent need for stronger security measures in I2I diffusion models.
