### Summary

This paper introduces a novel method for band selection in hyperspectral imaging, leveraging the expectation maximization algorithm to achieve sparsity and inter-band relationship depiction. The authors provide theoretical proofs and experimental validation to support their claims, demonstrating the effectiveness of their approach in terms of classification accuracy and sparsity.

### Strengths

1. The paper presents a new approach to band selection in hyperspectral imaging that utilizes the expectation maximization algorithm, which is a unique contribution to the field.
2. The authors provide theoretical proofs to support the effectiveness of their method, which adds a layer of rigor to their work.

### Weaknesses

#### comment

4. The paper also lacks a discussion of the limitations of the proposed method and potential directions for future research.
