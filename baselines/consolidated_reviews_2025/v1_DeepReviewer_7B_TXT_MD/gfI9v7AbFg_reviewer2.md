### Summary

This paper proposes STRATEGIST, a framework that combines LLMs with RL to improve strategic decision-making in multi-agent environments. It uses LLMs to generate high-level strategies and RL for low-level policy refinement, achieving better performance in complex games like GOPS and Avalon compared to traditional RL and other LLM-based agents.
