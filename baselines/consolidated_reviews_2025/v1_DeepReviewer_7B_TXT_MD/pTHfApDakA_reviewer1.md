### Summary

The paper proposes a method to improve multi-step reasoning in LLMs by verifying each step of a solution attempt. The method uses the LLM to extract the target of a given step and regenerate an alternative solution to the step, given the previous steps. The original solution and the alternative solution are then compared to determine if the original solution was correct. The method is tested on three datasets: GSM8K, MathQA, and MATH, showing improved accuracy compared to baseline methods.
