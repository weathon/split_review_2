The authors also develop an organoid intelligence expansion platform based on spiking neural network to explore organoid-machine collaborative intelligence. ### Suggestions

The authors should provide a more detailed explanation of the specific neural computing models used in the simulation framework. While the paper mentions 'advanced neural computing models,' it lacks specifics on the architecture, activation functions, and training procedures. For example, are they using recurrent neural networks, convolutional neural networks, or a combination? What are the specific parameters used for these models? How are the neurons and synapses represented within the simulation? Providing these details would allow for a better understanding of the simulation's underlying mechanisms and enable reproducibility. Furthermore, the authors should clarify how the simulation handles the temporal dynamics of neural activity. Is it using discrete time steps, and if so, what is the time step size? How does the model capture the propagation delays and synaptic transmission times? Addressing these points would significantly enhance the technical depth of the paper.

The benchmark dataset, while valuable, could benefit from a more rigorous analysis of its limitations. The authors should discuss the potential biases in the real-world organoid data and how these biases might affect the evaluation of the simulation framework. For instance, are there specific types of interactions or patterns in the data that are underrepresented? How does the benchmark account for the variability in real-world organoid experiments? Additionally, the authors should consider including a wider range of evaluation metrics beyond those currently used. Metrics such as temporal precision, phase locking value (PLV), or coherence could provide a more comprehensive assessment of the simulation's accuracy and biological relevance. A more thorough discussion of the benchmark's limitations and potential improvements would strengthen the paper's contribution.

Finally, the authors should elaborate on the specific mechanisms of the organoid intelligence expansion platform. How does the spiking neural network (SNN) interact with the simulation framework? Is the SNN trained to predict or classify the neural activity patterns generated by the simulation? What are the specific learning rules used in the SNN? How does the platform facilitate collaborative intelligence between the organoid simulation and the SNN? Providing more details on the architecture, training process, and interaction mechanisms of the platform would be crucial for understanding its potential applications and limitations. Furthermore, the authors should discuss the scalability of the platform and its applicability to more complex organoid simulations. Addressing these points would enhance the paper's impact and provide a more complete picture of the proposed framework.
