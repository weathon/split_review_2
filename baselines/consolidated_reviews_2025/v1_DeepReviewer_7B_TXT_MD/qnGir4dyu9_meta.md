## Summary

This paper introduces RACCooN, a video-to-video editing framework designed to facilitate various manipulation tasks, including object removal, addition, and modification, by leveraging multimodal large language models (MLLMs). The framework operates in two distinct stages: the video-to-paragraph (V2P) stage and the paragraph-to-video (P2V) stage. In the V2P stage, a pre-trained Video-LLM (PG-Video-LLaVA) is employed to generate detailed, object-centric paragraphs from input videos. This is achieved through a multi-granular spatiotemporal pooling strategy, which aims to capture both local and global visual features, resulting in more accurate and informative descriptions. The generated paragraphs then serve as the basis for the P2V stage, where a video diffusion model is conditioned on these paragraphs to guide the video editing process. The authors also introduce a new dataset, VPLM, which contains video descriptions and object masks, to facilitate the training and evaluation of the proposed framework. The paper presents extensive experimental results, demonstrating the effectiveness of RACCooN in various video editing tasks and highlighting its potential as a versatile tool for video manipulation. The core contribution of this work lies in the integration of a video-to-paragraph stage with a paragraph-to-video stage, allowing for user-friendly textual descriptions of video content and enabling precise video editing based on these descriptions. The authors also introduce a novel multi-granular spatiotemporal pooling strategy to generate detailed paragraphs, which is a key component of their framework. The paper's empirical findings suggest that RACCooN can effectively perform object removal, addition, and modification, and that the generated paragraphs are of high quality. The introduction of the VPLM dataset is also a valuable contribution to the field, providing a resource for training and evaluating video editing models. Overall, the paper presents a well-structured and innovative approach to video editing, with a focus on user-friendliness and the ability to perform complex manipulation tasks. However, as I will discuss in the following sections, there are several limitations that need to be addressed to further improve the framework's performance and applicability.
