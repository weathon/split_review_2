### Summary

This paper presents a novel approach to enhance the mathematical reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) through a bootstrapping method. The authors introduce MetaMathQA, a dataset generated by creating new question variations and reasoning paths from existing mathematical problems. By fine-tuning LLaMA-2 on this dataset, they develop MetaMath, which achieves state-of-the-art performance on GSM8K and MATH benchmarks, outperforming other open-source LLMs.
