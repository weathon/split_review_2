## Summary

This paper introduces a novel deep learning-based method, termed AI2TA, for phishing attack localization within email communications. The core objective is to identify the most important sentences within an email that are indicative of a phishing attempt, thereby facilitating a deeper understanding of the attack and enabling more effective countermeasures. The proposed method leverages an information-theoretic perspective and employs a variational information bottleneck (VIB) approach to achieve this goal. Specifically, the method involves training a selection network to identify crucial sentences and a phishing classifier to predict the likelihood of phishing. A key aspect of the approach is the use of a cross-entropy loss function to train the selection network, which is designed to encourage the selection of sentences that are most informative for phishing detection. The authors introduce two novel metrics, Label-Accuracy and Cognitive-True-Positive, to evaluate the performance of their method. Label-Accuracy measures the accuracy of the selected sentences in reflecting phishing-related information, while Cognitive-True-Positive assesses the alignment of the selected sentences with known cognitive triggers used in phishing emails. The empirical evaluation of AI2TA is conducted on seven diverse real-world email datasets, and the results demonstrate that the proposed method outperforms five state-of-the-art interpretable machine learning approaches in terms of both accuracy and cognitive-true-positive metrics. The authors emphasize the importance of their work in the context of email security, highlighting the potential of their method to improve phishing detection and localization. Overall, the paper presents a significant contribution to the field of phishing attack localization by introducing a novel method, new metrics, and extensive empirical validation. However, as I will discuss, there are several areas where the paper could be strengthened, particularly in terms of the depth of explanation and the thoroughness of the analysis.
