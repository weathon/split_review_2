### Summary

This paper proposes a method to detect machine-generated text from proprietary models using white-box detection techniques. The authors introduce Probability Distribution Estimation (PDE), which estimates the full probability distribution from partial observations provided by the API. By applying PDE to existing white-box methods, they achieve significant improvements in detection accuracy across various proprietary models and languages.
