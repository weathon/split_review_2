### Summary

This paper studies the mean field control (MFC) problem on large sparse graphs with finite expected degree and infinite second moment. The authors propose a novel model called Cooperative Mean Field Games on Chung-Lu Graphs (CLCMFGs) and design scalable learning algorithms for it. The authors provide a two systems approximation and prove its convergence. The authors also provide a detailed theoretical analysis and motivation for the model. The authors evaluate their model and algorithms on synthetic and real-world datasets and compare them to existing methods.
