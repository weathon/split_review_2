## Summary

This paper introduces a novel approach for reconstructing high-quality, watertight manifold meshes from multi-view images, addressing a significant challenge in 3D reconstruction. The core idea is to combine the strengths of neural field rendering and differentiable rasterization-based mesh extraction. The method begins by employing a neural field representation, specifically a TensoRF, to obtain an initial geometry and appearance. This is achieved through differentiable volume rendering, which allows for the optimization of the neural field parameters. Subsequently, the method leverages a differentiable marching cubes algorithm, termed DiffMC, to extract a mesh from the rendered geometry. This is followed by a refinement stage where the mesh geometry and appearance are further optimized using a similar pipeline to NeRF2. The key innovation here is the use of DiffMC, which is a faster and more efficient implementation of the marching cubes algorithm, tailored for differentiable computation. The method is evaluated on several datasets, including NeRF-Synthetic, MipNeRF-360, and LLFF, demonstrating its ability to produce high-quality meshes with intricate details. The authors also explore the method's compatibility with existing graphics pipelines by deploying it on GLSL, showcasing its potential for real-time rendering applications. The results indicate that the proposed method achieves state-of-the-art performance in terms of both mesh quality and rendering fidelity, outperforming existing approaches. The paper's contribution lies in the effective integration of neural field rendering and differentiable rasterization, providing a robust and efficient method for high-quality mesh reconstruction. The use of DiffMC is a significant technical innovation, addressing the computational bottleneck associated with the marching cubes algorithm. The paper is well-written and easy to follow, making the technical details accessible. Overall, this work presents a valuable contribution to the field of 3D reconstruction, offering a practical and effective solution for generating high-quality meshes from multi-view images.
