## Summary

This paper introduces a novel approach to counterfactual explanation (CFE) for complex models, particularly focusing on explainable NLP models. The core contribution lies in two distinct methods for generating counterfactuals: a generative approach utilizing large language models (LLMs) and a matching approach based on learning a causal embedding space. The authors also present a new benchmark dataset, CEBaB, which comprises human-annotated, high-quality counterfactuals for model explanation tasks. The generative method leverages LLMs to produce counterfactuals by prompting the model to generate text that adheres to specific causal relationships. The matching method, on the other hand, involves learning a latent embedding space that aligns with causal graphs, enabling efficient matching of CFs. The authors evaluate their methods on the CEBaB dataset, demonstrating that both approaches achieve state-of-the-art performance in model explanation. The paper also includes a theoretical analysis of the faithfulness of CF-based explanations, which is a crucial aspect of model interpretability. The empirical results show that the proposed methods, particularly the matching approach, offer a promising avenue for generating high-quality counterfactuals for model explanation. The paper's significance lies in its contribution to the field of model explanation by providing a new benchmark dataset and two novel methods for generating counterfactuals. The use of LLMs for CF generation and the learning of causal embedding spaces are innovative approaches that have the potential to advance the field. The paper also provides a theoretical foundation for the faithfulness of CF-based explanations, which is essential for ensuring the reliability of model explanations. Overall, this paper makes a valuable contribution to the field of explainable AI by providing a new benchmark and two novel methods for generating counterfactual explanations, which are crucial for understanding the behavior of complex models. The theoretical analysis of faithfulness further strengthens the paper's contribution by providing a solid foundation for the proposed methods. The empirical results on the CEBaB dataset demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed methods, highlighting their potential for practical applications in model explanation.
