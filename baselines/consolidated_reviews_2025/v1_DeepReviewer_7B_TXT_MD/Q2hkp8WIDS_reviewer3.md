### Summary

This paper proposes a new model-based reinforcement learning algorithm, OC-STORM, which uses object-centric representations to improve sample efficiency in visually complex environments. The proposed algorithm first annotates key objects in a few frames using segmentation masks, extracts object features using a pre-trained foundation vision model, and then incorporates these object features with the raw observations to predict environmental dynamics. The policy is then trained over simulated trajectories generated by the world model. The proposed algorithm is evaluated on the Atari 100k benchmark and the visually complex game Hollow Knight. The results show that OC-STORM outperforms the baseline STORM on 18 out of 26 Atari games and achieves the best-known sample efficiency on several Hollow Knight bosses.
