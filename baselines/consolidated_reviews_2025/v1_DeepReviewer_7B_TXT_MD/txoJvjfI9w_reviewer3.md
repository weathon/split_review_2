### Summary

This paper proposes a novel Permutation-resilient learning (PEARL) framework to enhance the robustness of large language models (LLMs) against demonstration permutations in in-context learning (ICL). The framework leverages distributional robust optimization (DRO) to optimize the model's performance against the worst-case permutations. The authors introduce a hard permutation mining network (P-Net) to identify challenging permutations and use an entropy-constrained Sinkhorn algorithm to generate permutations. The PEARL framework is evaluated on LLaMA-3 and instruction tuning tasks, demonstrating significant improvements in both average and worst-case performance.
