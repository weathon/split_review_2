### Summary

This paper introduces AdvI2I, a novel framework designed to manipulate image-to-image diffusion models to generate NSFW content. The authors propose a method that extracts adversarial perturbations from images and uses them to induce the generation of NSFW content, even when safety filters are in place. They also introduce an adaptive version of the framework, AdvI2I-Adaptive, which aims to evade existing defense mechanisms. The paper evaluates the effectiveness of both frameworks on two state-of-the-art diffusion models, InstructPix2Pix and SDv1.5-Inpainting, demonstrating their potential to bypass current safety measures and highlighting the urgent need for stronger security measures in generative models.
