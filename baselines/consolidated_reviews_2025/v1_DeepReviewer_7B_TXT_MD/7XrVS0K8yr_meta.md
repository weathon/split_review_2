## Summary

The paper introduces Secure Floating (FLOATING), a framework designed to enhance the trustworthiness of mobility data from connected and autonomous vehicles (CAVs) and other mobile devices in real-time. FLOATING leverages federated learning and blockchain technology to ensure data privacy and integrity. The framework employs lightweight Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC) and robust verifiable federated learning (VFL) to allow nodes to collaborate and validate each other's trajectory data without compromising sensitive information. FLOATING is evaluated using real-world data from New York City, involving up to 8,000 nodes, and demonstrates promising results in terms of accuracy, privacy preservation, robustness, and scalability. The core contributions of the paper include a novel approach to verifying the trustworthiness of mobility data, a practical implementation using real-world data, and a comprehensive evaluation that shows the framework's effectiveness. However, the paper could benefit from a more detailed discussion of specific threats and vulnerabilities, a thorough analysis of the system's performance under various conditions, and a more comprehensive comparison with existing approaches. Despite these limitations, FLOATING represents a significant step towards ensuring the reliability and trustworthiness of mobility data in the context of connected and autonomous vehicles.
