### Summary

This paper proposes a model-based RL method that combines recent advances in computer vision with reinforcement learning. The method consists of four steps: 1) annotate key objects in a small number of frames using segmentation masks, 2) extract object features using a pre-trained foundation vision model, 3) incorporate these object features with the raw observations to predict environmental dynamics, and 4) train a policy using imagined trajectories generated by the world model. The proposed method, OC-STORM, is evaluated on the Atari 100k benchmark and the visually complex game Hollow Knight. The results show that OC-STORM outperforms the baseline STORM on 18 out of 26 Atari games and achieves the best-known sample efficiency on several Hollow Knight bosses.
