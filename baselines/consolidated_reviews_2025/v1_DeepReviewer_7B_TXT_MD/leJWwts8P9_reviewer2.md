### Summary

This paper proposes a statistical test to determine if two LLMs were trained independently or not. The test works by comparing the weight and activation distributions of two trained models and generating p-values by comparing the similarity of the two trained models with that of several independent re-trained models. The method is evaluated on a set of 21 open-weight models and is shown to reliably identify fine-tuned pairs of models.
