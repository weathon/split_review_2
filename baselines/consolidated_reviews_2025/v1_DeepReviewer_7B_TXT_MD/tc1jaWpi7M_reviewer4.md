### Summary

This paper proposes a new method for object completion, called MaskComp. MaskComp is a diffusion-based method that iteratively refines the mask and the generated object. The mask is refined by a mask denoiser and the object is generated by a conditional diffusion model. The paper shows that MaskComp outperforms other methods on two object completion datasets.
