### Summary

The paper proposes a new ANN architecture called Cyclic NN, which is inspired by the complex, graph-like neural networks found in biological intelligence. Unlike traditional ANNs that are structured as layered feedforward networks, Cyclic NNs can have neurons connected in any graph-like structure, allowing for more flexible and dynamic interactions between neurons. The authors compare their method to the Forward-Forward algorithm and show that their method outperforms it.
