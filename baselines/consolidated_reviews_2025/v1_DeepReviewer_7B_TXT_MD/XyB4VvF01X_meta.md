## Summary

This paper introduces Graph2Tac, a novel graph neural network (GNN) model designed to enhance theorem proving within the Coq proof assistant. The core idea is to leverage a graph-based representation of Coq terms and definitions, enabling the model to adapt to new concepts and theorems in real-time. The authors construct a graph where nodes represent terms, including types, and edges represent relationships between these terms. This graph is built from a large dataset of Coq terms and definitions collected from the Heq Robinson packages. The GNN is trained on this dataset to learn representations of terms and definitions, which are then used to predict the correctness of theorems. The model is trained on a graph-based dataset of Coq terms and definitions, and can adapt to new definitions and theorems in real-time. The authors compare their model with other symbolic and machine learning solvers, and show that Graph2Tac outperforms these solvers in proving theorems in new Coq packages. The paper presents a comprehensive comparison of Graph2Tac with other symbolic and machine learning solvers, including CoqHammer, k-NN, and Transformer-GPU. The results demonstrate that Graph2Tac performs well in proving theorems in new Coq packages.
