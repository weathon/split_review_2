## Summary

This paper introduces OmniSep, a novel framework for sound separation that leverages multi-modal queries, including text, images, and audio, to achieve open-vocabulary sound separation. The core contribution lies in the development of a unified model capable of handling diverse query types, moving beyond the limitations of single-modal approaches. The authors propose a Query-Mixup strategy, which mixes embeddings from different modalities during training, enabling the model to optimize each modality concurrently and achieve a unified sound separation model. This strategy is further enhanced by a negative query approach, designed to remove unwanted sound information, and a Query-Augment approach, which allows for open-vocabulary sound separation using unrestricted natural language descriptions. The experimental results, conducted on the VG-Sound and MUSIC datasets, demonstrate that OmniSep achieves state-of-the-art performance in sound separation tasks across different modalities. The authors also present qualitative analysis to support their claims.
