### Summary

This paper introduces a new method for sampling from the posterior distribution of a flow-based generative model. The method, called ESS-Flow, is based on the idea of performing elliptical slice sampling in the latent space of the model. The authors show that this approach can be used to sample from the posterior distribution without the need for gradient information, which can be computationally expensive to compute. The authors also propose a multi-fidelity extension of the method, which can be used to improve the efficiency of the sampling process. The authors demonstrate the effectiveness of their method on a number of different tasks, including material generation and protein structure prediction.
