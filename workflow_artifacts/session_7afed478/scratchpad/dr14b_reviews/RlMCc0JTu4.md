### Summary

This paper proposes a framework named Tactile Affordance in Robot Synesthesia (TARS) to integrate visual and tactile information for manipulation tasks. The framework consists of two main components: the Visual-Tactile Affordance (VTA) module, which predicts affordances based on visual and tactile data, and the Visual-Tactile Policy (VTP) module, which uses these affordances to make manipulation decisions.
