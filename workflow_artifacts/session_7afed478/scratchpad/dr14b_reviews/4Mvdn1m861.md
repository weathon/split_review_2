### Summary

This paper proposes a training-free object counting method based on the Segment Anything Model (SAM). The method consists of two key components: Probabilistic Prompt Generation and Output Token-based Verification. The probabilistic prompt generation stage efficiently generates prompts based on probability distributions derived from SAM’s image embedding, while the output token-based verification stage uses SAM’s output tokens to effectively distinguish between positive and negative instances. The method achieves competitive performance on the FSC-147 and CARPK datasets, demonstrating its effectiveness in counting objects without the need for training.
