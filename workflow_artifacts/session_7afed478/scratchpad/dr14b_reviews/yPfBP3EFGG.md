### Summary

This paper proposes a Stochastic Two-track Nesterov-accelerated Adaptive Momentum Estimation (STNAdam) algorithm for solving nonconvex composite optimization problems. The algorithm uses a two-track iteration framework, which involves an extrapolation track and a regular update track, to explore a better iteration direction. Under the Kurdyka-Łojasiewicz property, the authors prove that the sequence generated by STNAdam almost surely converges to a stationary point. Experimental results on low-light image enhancement demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.
